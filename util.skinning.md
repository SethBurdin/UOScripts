Script premise: This script will run with the expection of the player riding a rideable beetle that can be used as a pack to transfer goods to. The script will detect nearby corpses, attempt to skin them, and then drag the leather to the mount's inventory. 

Step 1. Scan corpses and find if they are able to be skinned or not.
Step 2. Use dagger or skinning knife to skin nearby corpse.
Step 3. Identify the players beatle.
Step 4. Transfer the leather on the corpse to the beatle.
